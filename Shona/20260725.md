Vhidhiyo yevakuru ndeimwe yezvikamu zvedhijitari zvemukati zvakanyanya kukanganiswa nehumbavha.



Vagadziri, masitudiyo, uye mapuratifomu enhengo anowanzo kuisa mari yakawanda mukugadzira, kugadzirisa, kugovera, uye mashandiro evatengi. Asi kana faira revhidhiyo rakajairwa raendeswa kune vashandisi, rinogona kutorwa, kuendeswa mberi, kurekodha skrini, kurodha zvakare, kana kurongedzerwa uye kutengeswazve nevashambadzi vasina mvumo.



Kune mabhizinesi ezvinyorwa zvevakuru, piracy harisi dambudziko remigwagwa chete. Inokuvadza zvakananga mari, inoderedza kukosha kwenhengo dzakabhadharwa, uye inoderedza kukosha kwekutengesa kwenguva refu kweraibhurari yezvinyorwa.

## Nei piracy yakajairika muvhidhiyo yevakuru



### 1. Mafaira akabhadharwa ari nyore kugovera



Vhidhiyo yezvinyorwa zvevakuru zvinowanzo tengeswa kuburikidza nekutenga kamwe chete, zvirongwa zvekunyoresa, nharaunda dzakavanzika, kana masumbu akasarudzika. Pasina encryption uye mvumo yekudzora, mutengi anogona kutumira faira kumapoka ekutaura, madhiraivha egore, maforamu, kana mawebhusaiti ehumbavha.



Kamwe faira rikapararira, zvinova zvakaoma kutsvaga uye zvinenge zvisingaite kudzora.



### 2. Screen kurekodha yakachipa



Kunyangwe kurodha kwakadzimwa, chero chinhu chinogona kuridzwa chinogona kubatwa ne-screen-rekodha maturusi kana chaiwo nharaunda. Mavhidhiyo akawanda akaburitswa haana kubiwa kubva kumaseva; vanokopwa panguva yekutamba.



Ichi ndicho chikonzero chekudzivirira-chikamu chevatambi chakakosha.



### 3. Nzvimbo dzekubira dzinogona kuita pundutso nekukurumidza



Zvemukati zvevakuru zvine mutengo wepamusoro wetraffic pawebhusaiti dzepiracy. Vatengesi vasina mvumo vanogona kuunganidza mavhidhiyo akabhadharwa uye kuita mari kuburikidza nemashambadziro, nhengo, kudzoserwa kwetraffic, kana nharaunda dzekugovera mafaera.



Izvi zvinoreva kuti vagadziri vepakutanga vanogona kurasikirwa nemari yavo isati yatombowana mutengo wayo wekugadzira.



### 4. Yakajairika vhidhiyo kutumira inoshaya mvumo yekudzora

- mudziyo upi unogona kuvhura zvirimo

- kana kutamba pasina Indaneti kuchibvumirwa
- ingave yekurekodha skrini, kugadzirisa, kana mashini chaiwo anofanira kuvharwa



Pasina izvi zvinodzora, kuburitswa kwemukati kunoburitswa kubva pakutanga.

## Zvakanakira encrypting vakuru vhidhiyo zvemukati



Encryption inobatsira kuderedza kugovana kusingatenderwe uye inoramba ichibhadhara vashandisi mukati meiyo yepamutemo yekutenga nzira.



Inodzivirirawo hurongwa hwenhengo. Kana mavhidhiyo akabhadharwa achigona kugovaniswa pachena, kukosha kwenhengo kunodonha nekukurumidza. Nekutumirwa kwakavharidzirwa uye mitemo yemvumo, nhengo-chete zvemukati zvinogara padyo nevateereri vakatarisirwa.



Encryption inoitawo kuti kutumira kune hunyanzvi. Kune masitudiyo, vagadziri, uye vanotengesa zvemukati, kushandisa yakazvitsaurira yakavanzika mutambi kwakavimbika pane kutumira akajairwa vhidhiyo mafaera.
