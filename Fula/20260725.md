# Ko fiiɗo mawniraaɓe waɗi sokla kisal gollal gollal ngam gannugol

Fiiɗo mawniraaɓe ko e dow leƴƴi loowdi dijitaalɗi ɓurɗi heɓde caɗeele e piratiri.

Waɗɓe, studiooji e jokkondiral kuugal no njogii jawdi heewndi e waɗugol, taƴugol, yeeynugol e toppitagol jeyaaɓe. Kono so fiilde fiiɗo feere ndee yettaama kuutoro, nde waawi aawteede, neldude, ndeentaade e hello, ɓamtaade kadi, walla hay waɗeede pakkere nde yeeyaa ko alaa yamiroore.

E jeyɓe loowdi mawniraaɓe, piratiri wonaa tan caɗeele traffic. O bonna jaɓɓude jawdi, o lesɗina njaru limgal jeyal cuɓoraaɗo, o lesɗina kadi njaru kuugal juutiɗo ɗum fiilde loowdi.

---

## Hol ko waɗi piratiri heewde e fiiɗo mawniraaɓe

### 1. Fiilde soodiinde no weeɓi lollinde

Fiiɗo mawniraaɓe no yeeyee e soodugol gootol, subscription, dental suturo, walla pakkere cuɓoraaɗo. So alaa gannugol e ƴeewde yamiroore, jeyaaɗo waawi neldude fiilde ndee e dental chat, cloud drive, forum, walla lowre piratiri.

So fiilde ndee lolli, rewindaade nde no metti, jogoraade nde kadi no ɓuri mettaade.

### 2. Ndeentagol hello no hayni njoddi

Hay so aawtagol daaƴaama, kala ko waawi tarde waawi heɓteede e kuutorgal ndeentagol hello walla e windorde virtual. Fiiɗooji heewɗi ɗi ɓanngi wonaa e server ɗi wujjaaka; ɗi ndeentaa e sahaa tarde.

Ɗuum waɗi kisal e fannu player no teeŋtini.

### 3. Lowe piratiri no waawi heɓde njoddi jaasi

Loowdi mawniraaɓe jogii njaru traffic mawɗo e lowe piratiri. Yeeyooɓe ɓe alaa yamiroore mbaawi renndude fiiɗooji soodaaɗi, ɓe njogora ɗi e reklam, limgal, yiltude traffic, walla dental lollinde fiilde.

Ɗuum firti ko waɗɓe asli mbaawi majjude jawdi hade ɓe nduttaade njoddi waɗugol.

### 4. Yeeynugol fiiɗo feere alaa ƴeewde yamiroore

Fiilde MP4 feere waawataa ƴeewde no moƴƴi:

- hol masiŋ ka waawi udditde loowdi
- hol nde jottondiral gasata
- hol noone fiiɗo waawi tarde
- so tarde e offline yamiraama
- so ndeentagol hello, debugging walla virtual machine ina foti haɗeede

So ɗee ƴeewɗe alaa, yeeynugol loowdi woni e yaasi gila fuɗɗoode.

---

## Nafoore gannugol fiiɗo mawniraaɓe

Gannugol wallita e lesɗinde lollinde ko alaa yamiroore, tee waɗa kuutorɓe yoɓɓe njokka laawol soodugol laawol.

O kisna kadi yuɓɓo limgal. So fiiɗooji soodaaɗi mbaawi lolleede no feewi, njaru limgal no yaha les jaasi. E yeeynugol gannaaɗo e laabi yamiroore, loowdi limgal tan ɓurata heddude e yimɓe ɗum waɗanaa.

Yeeynugol kadi ɓurata wonde gollal gollal. E studiooji, waɗɓe e yeeyooɓe suturo, player gannaaɗo cuɓoraaɗo ɓuri hoolaade e neldude fiilde fiiɗo feere.
