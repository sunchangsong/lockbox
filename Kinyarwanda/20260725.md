## Kuki piratage ikunze kugaragara muri videwo ikuze



### 1. Amadosiye yishyuwe biroroshye kugabana



Abakuze ba videwo bakuze akenshi bagurishwa binyuze mubigura rimwe, gahunda yo kwiyandikisha, imiryango yigenga, cyangwa bundle yihariye. Hatabayeho kugenzura no kugenzura uburenganzira, umukiriya arashobora kohereza dosiye mumatsinda yo kuganira, ibicu bitwara ibicu, amahuriro, cyangwa imbuga za piratage.



Iyo dosiye imaze gukwirakwira, biragoye kuyikurikirana kandi ntibishoboka kugenzura.



### 2. Kwandika amashusho birahendutse



Ndetse iyo gukuramo byahagaritswe, ikintu cyose gishobora gukinishwa kirashobora gufatwa nibikoresho bifata amajwi cyangwa ibidukikije. Amashusho menshi yasohotse ntabwo yibwe muri seriveri; bakoporowe kurwego rwo gukina.



Iyi niyo mpamvu kurinda urwego rwabakinnyi bifite akamaro.



### 3. Urubuga rwibisambo rushobora kunguka vuba



Ibirimo abakuze bifite traffic traffic nyinshi kurubuga rwibisambo. Abagurisha batabifitiye uburenganzira barashobora kwegeranya videwo yishyuwe kandi bakayakoresha binyuze mumatangazo, abanyamuryango, kuyobora ibinyabiziga, cyangwa abaturage basangira dosiye.



Ibyo bivuze ko abarema umwimerere bashobora gutakaza amafaranga mbere yuko ibiyirimo bigarura igiciro cyayo.



### 4. Gutanga amashusho bisanzwe ntibigenzura uruhushya



Idosiye ya MP4 ntishobora kugenzura neza:



- ni ikihe gikoresho gishobora gufungura ibirimo
- igihe kwinjira bigomba kurangira
- ni kangahe amashusho ashobora gukinwa
- niba gukina kumurongo byemewe
- niba gufata amashusho, gukuramo, cyangwa imashini ziboneka bigomba guhagarikwa



Hatabayeho kugenzura, gutanga ibirimo bigaragazwa kuva mbere.

Irinda kandi gahunda yabanyamuryango. Niba videwo zishyuwe zishobora gusaranganywa kubuntu, agaciro k'abanyamuryango kagabanuka vuba. Hamwe nogusobeka gutanga no gutanga amategeko, abanyamuryango-bonyine baguma hafi yabateganijwe.



Encryption nayo itanga itangwa ryumwuga. Kuri sitidiyo, abayiremye, hamwe n’abagurisha ibintu byihariye, ukoresheje umukinnyi wabigenewe wabitswe neza kuruta kohereza dosiye zisanzwe.
