# Kungani Okuqukethwe Kwevidiyo Yabantu Abadala Kudinga Ukuvikelwa Kokubethelwa



Ividiyo yabantu abadala ingesinye sezigaba zokuqukethwe kwedijithali ezithinteka kakhulu ubugebengu.



Abadali, izitudiyo, nezinkundla zobulungu ngokuvamile batshala imali eningi ekukhiqizeni, ekuhleleni, ekusabalaliseni, nasekusebenzeni kwamakhasimende. Kodwa uma ifayela levidiyo elivamile lilethwa kubasebenzisi, lingadawunilodeka, lidluliselwe phambili, lirekhodwe ngesikrini, lilayishwe futhi, noma lipakishwe futhi lidayiswe kabusha abasabalalisi abangagunyaziwe.



Emabhizinisini abantu abadala, ubugebengu akuyona nje inkinga yethrafikhi. Ilimaza ngokuqondile imali engenayo, yenza buthaka inani lobulungu obukhokhelwayo, futhi yehlise inani lezentengiselwano lesikhathi eside lomtapo wokuqukethwe.



---



## Kungani ubugebengu buvame kangaka kuvidiyo yabantu abadala



### 1. Amafayela akhokhelwayo kulula ukuwabelana



Okuqukethwe kwevidiyo yabantu abadala kuvame ukuthengiswa ngokuthenga okukodwa, izinhlelo zokubhaliselwe, imiphakathi eyimfihlo, noma izinqwaba ezikhethekile. Ngaphandle kokulawula ukubethela nokugunyazwa, ikhasimende lingadlulisela ifayela kumaqembu ezingxoxo, amadrayivu amafu, izinkundla, noma amawebhusayithi obugebengu.



Uma ifayela selisabalala, kuba nzima ukulandelela futhi cishe kungenzeki ukulilawula.



### 2. Ukurekhoda kwesikrini kushibhile



Ngisho noma ukulanda kukhutshaziwe, noma yini engadlalwa isengathathwa ngamathuluzi okurekhoda isikrini noma izindawo ezibonakalayo. Amavidiyo aputshuziwe amaningi awabiwa kumaseva; zikopishwa esiteji sokudlala.



Yingakho ukuvikelwa kwezinga lomdlali kubalulekile.



### 3. Izindawo zokugebenga zingazuza ngokushesha



Okuqukethwe kwabantu abadala kunethrafikhi ephezulu kumawebhusayithi obugebengu. Abathengisi abangagunyaziwe bangase baqoqe amavidiyo akhokhelwayo futhi benze imali ngawo ngezikhangiso, ubulungu, ukuqondisa kabusha kwethrafikhi, noma imiphakathi yokwabelana ngamafayela.



Lokho kusho ukuthi abadali bangempela bangase balahlekelwe imali engenayo ngaphambi kokuba okuqukethwe kwabo kubuyise izindleko zakho zokukhiqiza.



### 4. Ukulethwa kwevidiyo okujwayelekile akunalo igunya lokulawula



Amafayela angenalutho we-MP4 awakwazi ukulawula ngokuthembekile:



- iyiphi idivayisi engavula okuqukethwe
- lapho ukufinyelela kufanele kuphelelwe yisikhathi
- ingadlalwa kangaki ividiyo
- noma ukudlala ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi kuvunyelwe
- noma ngabe ukurekhoda kwesikrini, ukulungisa iphutha, noma imishini ebonakalayo kufanele ivinjwe



Ngaphandle kwalezi zilawuli, ukulethwa kokuqukethwe kudalulwa kusukela ekuqaleni.

## Izinzuzo zokubethela okuqukethwe kwevidiyo yabantu abadala



Ukubethela kusiza ukwehlisa ukwabelana okungagunyaziwe futhi kugcina abasebenzisi bekhokha ngaphakathi kwendlela yokuthenga esemthethweni.



Iphinde ivikele uhlelo lobulungu. Uma amavidiyo akhokhelwayo angabiwa mahhala, inani lobulungu lehla ngokushesha. Ngemithetho yokulethwa ebethelwe kanye nokugunyazwa, okuqukethwe kwelungu kuphela kuhlala kuseduze nezithameli ezihlosiwe.



Ukubethela nakho kwenza ukulethwa kube okuchwepheshile. Ezitudiyo, abadali, nabathengisi bokuqukethwe okuyimfihlo, ukusebenzisa isidlali esibethelwe esizinikele kuthembeke kakhulu kunokuthumela amafayela evidiyo ajwayelekile.
