# Kungani ividiyo yabantu abadala idinga ukufihlwa?

Ukwebiwa kwamavidiyo abantu abadala kujwayelekile. Okuqukethwe okukhokhelwe kungalandwa, kuthunyelwe, kuqoshwe esikrinini futhi kuthengiswe futhi. LockBox isiza ukufihla ividiyo, ukulawula imvume yedivayisi, isikhathi sokuphelelwa, inani lokudlala nemvume ye-offline ukuze kuvikelwe imali engenayo nezimpahla zedijithali.
