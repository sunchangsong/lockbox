# Kutheni uMxholo weVidiyo yaBantu abadala ufuna uKhuseleko lokuFihla



Ividiyo yabantu abadala lelinye lamacandelo omxholo wedijithali achatshazelwa kakhulu bubuqhetseba.



Abadali, izitudiyo, kunye namaqonga obulungu bahlala betyala imali eninzi kwimveliso, ukuhlela, ukusasaza, kunye nokusebenza kwabathengi. Kodwa xa ifayile yevidiyo eqhelekileyo inikezelwe kubasebenzisi, inokukhutshelwa, ithunyelwe, irekhodwe isikrini, ilayishwe kwakhona, okanye iphinde ipakishwe kwaye ithengiswe ngokutsha ngabasasazi abangagunyaziswanga.



Kumashishini omxholo wabantu abadala, ubuqhetseba ayisiyongxaki yetrafikhi kuphela. Iyonakalisa ngokuthe ngqo ingeniso, yenza buthathaka ixabiso lobulungu obuhlawulweyo, kwaye inciphisa ixabiso lezorhwebo lexesha elide lelayibrari yomxholo.



---



## Kutheni ubuqhetseba buxhaphake kangaka kwividiyo yabantu abadala



### 1. Iifayile ezihlawulweyo kulula ukwabelana ngazo



Umxholo wevidiyo yabantu abadala uhlala uthengiswa ngokuthenga okukodwa, izicwangciso zobhaliso, uluntu lwabucala, okanye iinyanda ezikhethekileyo. Ngaphandle koguqulelo oluntsonkothileyo kunye nolawulo logunyaziso, umthengi unokuthumela ifayile kumaqela okuncokola, iidrive zamafu, iiforam, okanye iiwebhusayithi zobutsotsi.



Nje ukuba ifayile isasazeke, kuba nzima ukuyilandela kwaye phantse akunakwenzeka ukuyilawula.



### 2. Ukurekhoda kwesikrini kuyabiza



Naxa ukukhuphela kuvaliwe, nantoni na enokuthi idlalwe isenokuthinjwa ngezixhobo zokurekhoda kwesikrini okanye iindawo ezisingqongileyo. Iividiyo ezininzi ezivuzayo azibiwa kwiiseva; zikotshwa kwinqanaba lokudlala.



Kungenxa yoko le nto ukhuseleko lwenqanaba lomdlali lubalulekile.



### 3. Iindawo zobutsotsi zinokuzuza ngokukhawuleza



Umxholo wabantu abadala unexabiso eliphezulu letrafikhi kwiiwebhusayithi zobutsotsi. Abathengisi abangagunyaziswanga banokuqokelela iividiyo ezihlawulelweyo kwaye benze imali ngeentengiso, ubulungu, ukuhanjiswa kwetrafikhi, okanye ukwabelana ngeefayile uluntu.



Oko kuthetha ukuba abadali bokuqala banokuphulukana nengeniso ngaphambi kokuba umxholo wabo ubuyise iindleko zawo zemveliso.



### 4. Ukuhanjiswa kwevidiyo okuqhelekileyo akunalawulo lwemvume



Iifayile ze-MP4 ezingenanto azinakulawula ngokuthembekileyo:



- sesiphi isixhobo esinokuvula umxholo
- xa ukufikelela kufuneka kuphelelwe lixesha
- zingadlalwa kangaphi ividiyo
- nokuba ukudlala ngaphandle kweintanethi kuvumelekile
- nokuba ukurekhodwa kwesikrini, ukulungisa ingxaki, okanye oomatshini ababambekayo kufuneka bavalwe



Ngaphandle kolu lawulo, ukuhanjiswa komxholo kutyhilwa kwasekuqaleni.

## Iingenelo zokufihla umxholo wevidiyo yabantu abadala



Uguqulelo oluntsonkothileyo lunceda ukunciphisa ukwabelana okungagunyaziswanga kwaye ugcine ukuhlawula abasebenzisi ngaphakathi kwendlela yokuthenga esemthethweni.



Ikwakhusela nenkqubo yobulungu. Ukuba iividiyo ezihlawulelwayo zingabelana ngokukhululekileyo, ixabiso lobulungu liyehla ngokukhawuleza. Ngonikezelo olufihliweyo kunye nemithetho yogunyaziso, umxholo welungu kuphela uhlala usondele kubaphulaphuli ekujoliswe kubo.



Uguqulelo oluntsonkothileyo lukwenza unikezelo ngobungcali ngakumbi. Kwizitudiyo, abadali, kunye nabathengisi bomxholo wabucala, ukusebenzisa umdlali ozinikeleyo ofihliweyo kunokwethenjelwa ngakumbi kunokuthumela iifayile zevidiyo eziqhelekileyo.
