# Kubera iki amavidewo y'abakuze akeneye cane uburinzi bwo gupfundikira amakuru

Amavidewo y'abakuze ni kimwe mu bice vy'ibirimwo vya digitale bikunda kwibasirwa cane n'ubusuma bw'ibirimwo.

Abahinguzi, amasitudiyo n'ama platform y'abanyamuryango akunda gushiramwo amahera menshi mu guhingura, gutunganya, gukwiragiza no kwitaho abakiriya. Ariko iyo dosiye ya videwo isanzwe ishikirijwe umukoresha, irashobora gukururwa, kurungikwa abandi, gufatwa kuri ecran, gushirwa kuri internet kandi, canke no gupakirwa ikagurishwa ataruhusha.

Ku bakora ubucuruzi bw'ibirimwo vy'abakuze, ubusuma bw'ibirimwo si ikibazo c'uruja n'uruza gusa. Bugabanya inyungu mu buryo butaziguye, bugaca intege agaciro k'ubunyamuryango bwishurwa, kandi bugabanya agaciro k'igihe kirekire k'ububiko bw'ibirimwo.

---

## Kubera iki ubusuma bw'ibirimwo bukunda kuba bwinshi mu mavidewo y'abakuze

### 1. Dosiye zishuwe ziroroshe gusangira

Amavidewo y'abakuze akunda kugurishwa nk'ukugura rimwe, abonnement, umugwi wihariye, canke ipaki idasanzwe. Ata gupfundikira amakuru n'ugucungera uruhusha, umukiriya ashobora kurungika dosiye mu migwi yo kuganira, mu bubiko bwa cloud, ku ma forum canke ku mbuga z'ubusuma.

Iyo dosiye imaze gukwiragira, kuyikurikirana biragora, kuyigenzura bikaba hafi bidashoboka.

### 2. Gufata ecran ntibitwara amahera menshi

Naho gukurura dosiye vyoba vyugaye, ikintu cose gishobora gukinishwa gishobora gufatwa n'ibikoresho bifata ecran canke mu bidukikije vya virtual. Amavidewo menshi asohoka atavuye kuri server; yandukurwa igihe ariko arakina.

Ni co gituma uburinzi ku rwego rwa player buhambaye.

### 3. Imbuga z'ubusuma zirashobora kuronka inyungu ningoga

Ibirimwo vy'abakuze bifise agaciro kanini k'uruja n'uruza ku mbuga z'ubusuma. Abagurisha batabifitiye uruhusha barashobora kwegeranya amavidewo yishuwe bakayakuramwo inyungu biciye mu matangazo, ubunyamuryango, kuyobora traffic canke imigwi yo gusangira dosiye.

Ivyo bisigura ko abahinguzi ba mbere bashobora gutakaza inyungu batarasubizwa amahera yo guhingura.

### 4. Gutanga videwo bisanzwe nta cungerwa ry'uruhusha bifise

Dosiye za MP4 zisanzwe ntizishobora kugenzura neza:

- igikoresho gishobora gufungura ibirimwo
- igihe uburenganzira bwo kwinjira buherera
- incuro videwo ishobora gukinishwa
- nimba gukina offline vyemewe
- nimba gufata ecran, debugging canke virtual machines bikwiye guhagarikwa

Ata vyo kugenzura, gutanga ibirimwo biba biri mu kaga kuva mu ntango.

## Ivyiza vyo gupfundikira amavidewo y'abakuze

Gupfundikira amakuru bifasha kugabanya gusangira ataruhusha kandi bigumiza abakoresha bishura mu nzira yemewe yo kugura.

Biranakingira mfumo w'ubunyamuryango. Iyo amavidewo yishuwe ashobora gusangirwa ku buntu, agaciro k'ubunyamuryango karagabanuka ningoga. Ku gutanga ibirimwo bipfundikiwe n'amategeko y'uruhusha, ibirimwo vy'abanyamuryango gusa biguma hafi y'abavyagenewe.

Uburyo bwo gutanga nabwo buba ubwa kinyamwuga kurusha. Ku masitudiyo, abahinguzi n'abagurisha mu buryo bwihariye, player ipfundikiwe yihariye irizigirwa kurusha kurungika dosiye za videwo zisanzwe.

---

## Ivyiza vya LockBox

LockBox ni player ipfundikiwe n'igikoresho co gucungera uruhusha ku mavidewo n'ibirimwo vya digitale bifise agaciro kanini, harimwo amavidewo y'abakuze, amavidewo y'abanyamuryango gusa, ububiko bw'ibirimwo bwihariye, dosiye za PDF n'amashusho.

LockBox ireka abakoresha bemerewe bakaraba ibirimwo bisanzwe, ariko ikagora cane gukopa, kurungika abandi no gukwiragiza atagenzura.

Ivyiza nyamukuru:

- gupfundikira videwo
- kumenya gufata ecran
- uruhusha rwa code imwe ku gikoresho kimwe
- amatariki yo kurangira
- imbibe z'incuro zo gukina
- imbibe z'igitigiri c'ibikoresho
- uruhusha rwa offline
- uburinzi kuri virtual machines, debugging na injection
- gukina neza dosiye nini za HD, 4K n'amavidewo maremare

---

## Ni bande bashobora gukoresha LockBox?

LockBox ibereye:

- abahinguzi b'amavidewo y'abakuze
- amasitudiyo y'amavidewo y'abakuze
- ama platform y'ibirimwo vy'ubunyamuryango
- abagurisha ibirimwo vyishuwe mu buryo bwihariye
- abayobora imigwi yishurwa
- ama teams y'uburenganzira bwa digitale
- umuntu wese akeneye gukingira amavidewo yihariye canke afise agaciro kanini

## Incamake

Ubusuma bw'ibirimwo mu mavidewo y'abakuze ntibuzozimangana bwonyene. Iyo ibirimwo bitanzwe nka dosiye isanzwe ata burinzi bwo gukina, birashobora gukopwa, kurungikwa abandi no kugurishwa kandi.

LockBox ihuza gupfundikira ibirimwo, uburinzi bwo gukina, uruhusha rw'igikoresho no gucungera uburenganzira kugira ifashe ubucuruzi bw'ibirimwo kugabanya ingorane z'ubusuma no gukingira inyungu, ubunyamuryango n'itunga rya digitale ry'igihe kirekire.
